# v0.8 Commercial Visual Readiness Plan Audit

Date: September 23, 2026

Audited document:
`design/v0.8-commercial-visual-readiness-plan.md`

Verdict: **PASS WITH CORRECTIONS APPLIED**

## Audit scope

The plan was checked against:

- `design/v0.8-visual-mobile-overhaul-plan.md`
- `design/audits/v0.8-complete-visual-profitability-audit.md`
- `design/decisions/v0.8-live-3d-boundary.md`
- `design/audits/v0.8-baseline.md`
- `design/audits/v0.8-package-budget.md`
- `design/presentation_bible.md`

The independent review challenged commercial differentiation, proof-slice
scope, package allocation, score floors, mobile evidence, accessibility,
runtime-3D boundaries, autonomous execution, and storefront timing.

## Findings and corrections

### 1. Historical v0.6 package budget conflicted with v0.8 authority

Finding:

- `design/presentation_bible.md` contained a 12 MB added-PCK limit from the
  v0.6 remaster.
- The v0.8 plans use a measured 22 MiB target and 26 MiB hard ceiling.

Correction:

- The presentation bible now explicitly limits the 12 MB figure to v0.6.
- v0.8 measured package and stage budgets are the numeric authority.
- Art direction, motion, accessibility, renderer, light, particle, and audio
  constraints remain authoritative.

### 2. The proof slice retained the old locomotive-only ceiling

Finding:

- M3B pulls a Defense car, Heavy Cannon, two crew, Pursuer, environment slice,
  effects, and proof HUD into the old 7 MiB locomotive-only stage.

Correction:

- The final 22 MiB target remains unchanged.
- Intermediate ceilings are rebalanced to 5 / 10 / 15 / 19 / 21 / 22 MiB for
  M3A / M3B / M4 / M5 / M6 / M7-M8.
- M3B must report delta by asset category.
- If M3B exceeds 10 MiB, it must reduce invisible passes, frame sampling,
  atlas resolution, or nonessential environment breadth before scale-out.

### 3. Re-sequenced M4-M6 content lacked package reallocation

Finding:

- Cars, world, threats, and effects moved between milestones while the old
  stage ceilings remained unchanged.

Correction:

- Both v0.8 plans now use the same rebalanced cumulative ceilings.
- The final target and hard ceiling are not loosened.

### 4. Threat and boss personality was not a critical score

Finding:

- The prior audit identified threat animation and Longshadow spectacle as
  major commercial weaknesses and differentiators.
- The initial scorecard allowed that category to pass at 7/10.

Correction:

- Threat and boss personality is now critical with an 8/10 floor.

### 5. Signature loops had no final completion gate

Finding:

- The plan required a complete signature set but did not explicitly gate the
  full set at M7 or M9.

Correction:

- M7 requires all twelve loops to pass default and accessibility review.
- M9 regenerates all twelve from final release assets.
- The definition of done now names the complete set.

### 6. M3A could iterate indefinitely

Finding:

- The style-lock stage had no retry ceiling.

Correction:

- Three failed correction loops now force a new art-direction decision before
  more subjects are produced.

### 7. Mobile silhouette percentages were unsupported release constants

Finding:

- Initial 12% train and 7% threat height targets were not yet derived from the
  final style treatment.

Correction:

- They are now explicit M3A calibration targets.
- M3A records the accepted physical-pixel and percentage values.
- Later loosening requires side-by-side evidence of equal or better
  readability.

### 8. Hosted-device access was not named in the autonomy exceptions

Finding:

- Android-class evidence may require physical hardware or device-farm
  credentials.

Correction:

- Physical and hosted-device access and credentials are now explicit
  non-pipeline dependencies.

### 9. The signature-loop list counted phase transformations ambiguously

Finding:

- "Each Longshadow phase transformation" occupied one numbered line while
  representing three separate loops.

Correction:

- Veil, Tether, and Charge are now separate items.
- The final requirement is twelve loops, not an ambiguous ten.

### 10. Intermediate score normalization was implicit

Finding:

- "Applicable weighted score" did not define how absent later categories were
  treated.

Correction:

- Intermediate milestones re-normalize only represented categories.
- A category cannot become not applicable after its production scope begins.
- M3B scores Pursuer evidence for the critical threat category; M6 makes
  Longshadow mandatory for the full category score.

## Confirmed strengths

- Counts are consistent: six cars, twelve upgrades, three regular threats,
  four crew, and one three-phase boss.
- Score weights total 100.
- The proof slice precedes asset scale-out.
- The full runtime-3D rewrite remains rejected.
- Live 3D remains bounded by the accepted M0 decision record.
- Existing frame, draw, particle, texture, package, deterministic, fallback,
  and accessibility authorities are preserved.
- Storefront evidence begins in M3B instead of being deferred until release.
- The current v0.7 public build remains unchanged until the v0.8 release gate.

## Final authorization

The corrected plan is suitable to control M2-M9 implementation.

M2 may begin. M4 may not begin until the M3B proof slice reaches its critical
8/10 floors and passes the rebalanced 10 MiB cumulative package gate.
