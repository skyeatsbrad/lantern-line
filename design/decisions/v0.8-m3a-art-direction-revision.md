# v0.8 M3A art-direction revision

**Date:** September 24, 2026
**Status:** Accepted revision; style lock remains open

## Decision

Retain **Lantern Diorama** as the visual foundation, but revise the production
shape language from plausible miniature machinery to **exaggerated combat
silhouettes at gameplay scale**.

The first M3A correction budget produced a cohesive palette, camera, lighting
model, environment, HUD scale, and mobile composition. The final independent
gate agreed within 0.7 points in every category, but one critic still scored
train desirability at 7.2 and mobile readability at 7.3 because the Heavy
Cannon resembled a crane and the Rail Pursuer's six contact points collapsed at
thumbnail scale. Those are style-lock failures, not M3B animation or VFX work.

## Frozen foundation

- 6-degree orthographic camera pitch
- 11.5 orthographic scale and accepted gameplay framing
- cool dead-city depth against warm lantern light
- 1.8 px interior line and 2.4 px subject silhouette line
- semantic Coal-lit Ink & Brass palette
- current High Contrast relationship
- current render and mask pass library
- current desktop, compact, mobile, and thumbnail output sizes

## Revised silhouette rules

### Heavy Cannon

- The barrel remains forward-facing but drops to a shallow elevation.
- Barrel thickness and muzzle mass are exaggerated beyond realistic scale.
- A large recoil housing and split muzzle brake replace crane-like proportions.
- The silhouette must read as a weapon without HUD text at 320 pixels wide.

### Rail Pursuer

- Six legs terminate in six non-overlapping horizontal intervals.
- Middle legs splay farther from the body and remain thicker than decorative
  detail.
- The wedge head, hot eye, dorsal points, and tail hook remain secondary to
  the six-point contact silhouette.
- The silhouette must not read as a dog, gun carriage, or four-legged animal.

### Thumbnail HUD

- At 320 pixels wide, remove body copy that rasterizes as noise.
- Preserve the title mark, Focus state, and progress bar as the minimum useful
  identity and status fragment.

## Gate

Regenerate the selected treatment and repeat the two-critic gate. M3A closes
only when both critics score art direction, train desirability, mobile
readability, and performance at 7.5 or higher, with score spreads no greater
than 1.5 points.
