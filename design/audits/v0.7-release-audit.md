# v0.7 Gameplay Clarity Release Audit

Date: September 22, 2026

Verdict: **Published and verified.**

## Scope

This audit covers the v0.7 gameplay-clarity pass built on the published v0.6
presentation release:

- Route-control correctness.
- Physical-window responsive scaling and compact interface geometry.
- First-run and contextual onboarding.
- Continuous-repair and danger-route balance.
- Dense High Contrast label readability.
- Deterministic campaign and coordinator-level action coverage.
- Web and Windows package identity, metadata, and browser boot behavior.

## Independent review

An independent final-diff review found one actionable onboarding issue: the
in-run `AIM THE LANTERN` cinematic repeated the one-page quick start
immediately after the player dismissed it.

The duplicate cinematic was removed. Contextual teaching now begins with pace
after early travel, advances to power priorities after the first committed
route, and introduces detachment only when the rear car becomes critical. A
dedicated smoke regression protects this sequence.

The follow-up review found no other high-confidence functional, accessibility,
determinism, packaging, or release defects.

## Visual and accessibility review

Thirteen v0.7 captures cover:

- Desktop title and gameplay at 1280 x 720.
- 130% text, High Contrast combat, route, and Longshadow views at 960 x 540.
- Title, gameplay, route, and station views at a physical 844 x 390.

The review found and corrected two compact-HUD layout issues:

1. The resource panel could clip its second column at 844 x 390.
2. A long compact Longshadow status could expand the center panel over the

bottom controls, and a scrollable station with its departure action fixed in
view. High Contrast labels render after every enemy silhouette, ward, warning,
