v0.8 production.

agree within 0.3 points, the official one-command factory reproduces all

8.0 in every represented critical category before broad asset production may
begin.

## 1. Treatment comparison and correction history

The factory generated the same locomotive, Defense car, Heavy Cannon, Rail
Pursuer, headlight, dead-signal environment, and HUD fragment in three
treatments:

| Treatment | Pitch | Interior / silhouette line | Material direction |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Etched Brass | 0 degrees | 2.8 / 3.4 px | flatter, rougher, heavier ink |
| Lantern Diorama | 6 degrees | 1.8 / 2.4 px | balanced metal, warm lantern depth |
| Signal Noir | 10 degrees | 1.1 / 1.8 px | sharper rim, stronger value separation |

All treatments use the same semantic palette and gameplay composition. They
were judged at 1280 x 720, 960 x 540, 844 x 390, and 320 x 180 in default and
High Contrast presentation.

### Initial treatment correction

The first Etched Brass frame was too dark, had aggressive outlines, weak
headlight emphasis, oversized subjects, and a Pursuer that read as a pig-like
animal rather than a rail predator.

Corrections:

- expanded the orthographic world coverage
- rebuilt the scene as mobile gameplay rather than key art
- added layered industrial depth and a readable dead signal
- projected headlight and track anchors from Blender instead of hardcoding
  screen coordinates
- rebuilt the Pursuer as a low angular rail predator
- added locomotive, Defense car, Heavy Cannon, and environment detail
- split interior and outer-silhouette line treatments
- corrected comparison-sheet labels and spacing

### Reviewer ambiguity correction

The first two independent reviews disagreed between Signal Noir and Lantern
Diorama by more than the allowed 1.5 points in art direction and mobile
readability. The scene was revised to increase mobile action scale, strengthen
outer silhouettes, separate the Pursuer with a cool rim, and reduce background
competition.

Later review converged on Lantern Diorama but rejected the Heavy Cannon and
Pursuer silhouettes. The planned correction budget was exhausted, so M3A
stopped and recorded a formal direction revision before continuing, as
required by the milestone contract:

`design/decisions/v0.8-m3a-art-direction-revision.md`

### Final silhouette correction

The Heavy Cannon now uses a tank-like mantlet, solid breech, thick barrel,
stepped muzzle collars, recoil cylinder, and clear space before the locomotive.
The Rail Pursuer uses six separated rail-contact intervals that survive phone
and thumbnail reduction. Thumbnail HUD body copy was removed where it became
raster noise.

## Remaining boundary

M3A proves that the visual language, subject scale, asset factory, and evidence
pipeline are viable. It does not yet prove final animation, VFX, audio,
storefront framing, or integrated mobile playability at commercial quality.

M3B must apply this lock to a playable hero locomotive, Defense car, Heavy
Cannon, two crew members, Rail Pursuer, dead-signal environment, Focus,
critical damage, real detachment, and the visible proof HUD. Scale-out remains
blocked until that slice reaches the plan's 8.0 critical-category gates.

## Verdict

**PASS / GO TO M3B.**

Freeze Lantern Diorama and begin only the bounded M3B commercial proof slice.
Do not deploy public v0.8 and do not generate the remaining M4-M7 production
subjects until the M3B commercial gate passes.
