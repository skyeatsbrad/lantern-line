# v0.8 M3A style lock

**Date:** September 24, 2026
**Status:** Accepted and frozen for M3B production

## Decision

Use **Lantern Diorama** as the authored visual foundation for The Lantern
Line's v0.8 production art.

The treatment passed the M3A gate after the art-direction revision in
`v0.8-m3a-art-direction-revision.md`. The revision keeps the established
Coal-lit Ink & Brass identity while requiring exaggerated combat silhouettes
that survive gameplay and thumbnail scale.

This lock governs authored subjects, environments, baked lighting, sprite
passes, and profile relationships produced from M3B onward. It does not waive
the stricter M3B requirement for a final-quality playable proof slice scoring
at least 8.0 in every represented critical category.

## Camera and composition

| Property | Locked value |
|---|---:|
| Projection | Orthographic |
| Camera target | `[0.15, 0.0, 2.05]` |
| Camera distance | `15.0` |
| Camera pitch | `6 degrees` |
| Orthographic scale | `11.5` |
| Locomotive X | `0.75` |
| Defense car X | `-2.7` |
| Pursuer X | `4.35` |
| Dead signal X | `5.2` |
| Ground Z | `0.0` |

Required review sizes are 1280 x 720 desktop, 960 x 540 compact, 844 x 390
mobile, and 320 x 180 thumbnail. New authored subjects must be evaluated at
all four sizes in default and High Contrast presentation.

## Accepted mobile scale

The accepted 844 x 390 frame establishes the following physical release gate:

| Subject | Height | Width |
|---|---:|---:|
| Train | 25.128% | 38.981% |
| Rail Pursuer | 11.795% | 10.664% |

These values intentionally replace the original 12% train-height and 7%
regular-threat calibration targets for this Heavy Cannon proof frame. The
larger composition was required for the weapon and six-leg Pursuer silhouettes
to remain readable in independent phone and thumbnail review. Future work may
not reduce these accepted values without side-by-side evidence showing equal
or better gameplay readability.

The locomotive remains the dominant horizontal mass. Combat attachments use
large ownership shapes before plausible mechanical detail. The Heavy Cannon
must preserve its mantlet, solid breech, thick barrel, stepped muzzle collars,
and recoil cylinder. The Rail Pursuer must preserve six separated rail-contact
intervals, a low wedge head, dorsal points, hot eye, and tail hook.

## Palette mapping

The semantic palette remains:

- Night Void and Coal for world depth and unlit structural mass
- Iron and Slate for readable cool material groups
- Bone for text and high-value neutral detail
- Brass, Ember, and Flame for train ownership, heat, and active mechanisms
- Cold Signal for cool separation and signal technology
- Shadow Veil for Longshadow-only supernatural material
- Growth for recovery and repair
- Danger and Pursuer Rust for hostile warnings and Pursuer identity

No critical state may depend only on hue. Silhouette, value, text, iconography,
or timing must provide a second channel.

## Render passes and profile relationship

Every production subject supplies the passes that apply to its role:

- default and High Contrast beauty output
- silhouette
- subject mask
- mobile subject mask when scale validation is required
- part mask for modular assemblies
- normal and material data where lighting response is visibly useful

High uses atlases up to 2048 pixels with normal and material maps. Low uses
atlases up to 1024 pixels, omits normal and material maps, and applies a 1.15
outline scale. Medium and vector fallback preserve the same gameplay
information even when presentation detail is reduced.

## Deterministic production path

- Preview iteration uses Eevee Next.
- Canonical verification uses CPU Cycles, 12 samples, denoising, and bounded
  bounces.
- Blender 4.5.14 LTS is the pinned authoring version.
- The accepted preview set contains 46 review artifacts.
- Canonical CPU Cycles replay requires exact pixels.
- Preview replay requires exact pixels when stable. Hardware-sensitive Eevee
  output may use the recorded strict tolerance of at most 8 changed pixels,
  0.005% of an artifact, one channel level, and `0.00001` mean absolute
  channel delta.
- Review media and generated `.blend` files remain nonshipping evidence.
- No accepted output may depend on hand-editing a generated image or scene.

The official production entry point is:

```powershell
python tools\art\build_assets.py --category style --review
```

The official factory owns accepted preview evidence and rejects
`--engine canonical`. Canonical verification must use isolated paths:

```powershell
python tools\art\build_style_lock.py `
  --treatment lantern_diorama `
  --engine canonical `
  --verify-replay
```

`build_style_lock.py` automatically selects protected canonical work, media,
and receipt paths and rejects path overlap between preview and canonical
evidence. Explicit path overrides remain available only when they are
disjoint.

## Independent gate

| Reviewer | Art direction | Train desirability | Mobile readability | Performance |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Visual critic | 7.9 | 8.1 | 7.6 | 8.3 |
| Commercial critic | 8.2 | 8.0 | 7.6 | 8.6 |
| Median | 8.05 | 8.05 | 7.6 | 8.45 |
| Spread | 0.3 | 0.1 | 0.0 | 0.3 |

Every score exceeds the 7.5 M3A floor and every spread is below the 1.5-point
ambiguity threshold. Both critics confirmed that the Heavy Cannon reads as a
weapon and that the Pursuer's six contact points survive phone and thumbnail
scaling.
